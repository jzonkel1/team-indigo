The turn, in order

One crew, every trade, in the right sequence.

Most turn delays aren't quality problems — they're hand-off problems between vendors. When one company does every step, the painter isn't waiting on the drywall guy and the carpet isn't cleaned before the paint. Here's how a unit moves through Team Indigo.

1

Trashout & haul-off

Whatever the last tenant left goes out, and the unit is swept down so the trades can start.

2

Repairs & resurfacing

Drywall patched, and tubs, sinks, tile and countertops resurfaced instead of replaced.

3

Paint

Walls, ceilings, trim and doors. Whole-unit repaints or touch-ups, whichever the unit needs.

4

Full clean

Kitchen, bath, fixtures, windows and floors. Move-out grime gone before the carpet crew arrives.

5

Carpet, tile & grout

Hot-water extraction, pet treatment and tile restoration — the last trade in, so nothing gets tracked back over it.

6

Ready to lease

The unit is handed back on the date you gave us, ready to show. One invoice for the whole turn.

Don't need all six? Book the steps you're short on. Paint only, carpet only, or a clean between your own vendors — same crews, same schedule.
